## Scope

Storefront redesign, flagship surface: Home (`/`). Mode: Persuade. This brief's visual world extends to the rest of the storefront (Operate-mode pages: Shop, Product, Auctions, Cart, Checkout, Account) as an established world once the flagship lands, per the shared design system it establishes.

## Audience / job / action / proof / constraints

Aquarist hobbyists and breeders shopping Ellie's Friends (a real aquatics shop on the SellWithEllie platform). Job: decide whether to buy now or bid live. Action: land on a fixed-price listing or an active auction within seconds. Proof: tank-bred/quarantined claims, live bid activity, show-grade provenance. Constraint: preserve all current product data, copy, and terminology (content is fixed; layout, motion, and visual treatment are fully open).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalog is a specimen hall, not a product grid — every fish is an accessioned vitrine specimen with real provenance, refusing the soft-neutral SaaS-storefront default this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink-teal (#1b3a3f) and warm parchment (#f2ead9) ground, aged brass (#9c7a34) hardware accents, crimson (#8c2f2f) reserved only for the live/urgent state. A self-hosted, engraving-character serif for display names; a letterspaced small-caps sans for museum-label metadata (category, accession number, grade); a monospace for bid/price data set scoreboard-confident, not whispered.

STORY: A shopper walks a specimen hall, reads each vitrine's accession label (variety, grade, price), watches live acquisitions glow and tick in real time, and checks out feeling they acquired something considered — not just bought a listing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed vitrine wall — 3-4 specimen cards in soft cabinet-shadow depth, an accession-ledger strip above naming today's lot count and live-auction count, primary "Browse the collection" action anchored at the hero's base.

FORM: Assigned direction "The Specimen Cabinet" (natural-history specimen hall / engraved ichthyology plates), position 5 of 7 on the grounded resonance list, raised with scoreboard-confident bid/countdown numerals (from the seven-segment challenger), one genuinely glowing live-auction state (from the CRT-arcade challenger), and a named, fully distinct auction-lot state vocabulary — Scheduled / Live / Closed / Accessioned / Cancelled (from the orizuru-crane challenger). Seed key 7f38a07a, assigned index 5, mode persuade.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
